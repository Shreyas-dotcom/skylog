Everything about how you fly, worked out for you.

Log a flight by typing the airports and airline. Skylog fills in the distance, duration, time zones and logo, then updates every chart and record.

Route map and globe

Great-circle routes colored by how often you've flown them, or by alliance. Spin the globe, or replay your whole history flight by flight.

Records

Longest and fastest flights, biggest aircraft, busiest connecting airport, tightest connection, delays by airline and your airline of each year.

Alliances

See how your flying splits across Star Alliance, SkyTeam, oneworld and unaffiliated airlines, by flights, distance or time.

Data check

Catches impossible speeds, overlapping flights, duplicates, mis-logged return legs and times that don't match their time zones. Most fixes take one click.

Airline tail logos

Type "Qatar" or "Swiss" and Skylog finds the airline, its codes and its tail logo. 225 airlines are built in, and any other works with its code.

Stats that add up

Distance, time in the air, CO₂, countries and continents, seats, cabins and manufacturers, per year, month and weekday. Pick a year to see just that one.

PRIVACY

Encrypted before it ever leaves your browser.

Your site and repository are public, but your log isn't. Skylog turns your password into a key on your device and saves only ciphertext. GitHub stores it. Nobody else can read it, and there's no Skylog server in between.

1 · YOUR BROWSER

Your password becomes a key

PBKDF2-SHA256 · 600,000 rounds AES-256-GCM · fresh IV every save
2 · YOUR GITHUB REPO

Only ciphertext is saved

vault.json "data": "q7Fz0kVb3T9wYc2LmA8dR1xHnE5u…"

Every save is a git commit, so every version is kept.

3 · ANY DEVICE

Unlock it anywhere

Open your site on a laptop or phone and enter your password. Without it, visitors see a lock screen.

  • No Skylog accounts, servers or analytics.
  • Your GitHub token is stored inside the encrypted log.
  • Offline? Changes wait on your device, encrypted, until GitHub confirms.
  • Edited on two devices at once? Skylog asks which version to keep.

SETUP · ABOUT 5 MINUTES

Four steps, no installs.

All you need is a free GitHub account. On any other device, open the same address and enter your password.

  1. 01

    Fork the repository

    One click on GitHub gives you your own copy of Skylog.

  2. 02

    Turn on GitHub Pages

    Settings → Pages → deploy from main, folder / (root).

  3. 03

    Make an access token

    A fine-grained token for this one repo, with Contents: Read and write.

  4. 04

    Open your site

    Choose a password, paste the token, and pick your myFlightradar24 CSV if you have one.

Questions

What if I forget my password?

There's no reset. Your log is encrypted with a key made from your password, so nobody can open it without it, including GitHub and the people who made Skylog. Keep it in a password manager.

Can anyone see my flights?

No. Your repository is public, but the log is stored only as encrypted data. Visitors can tell a log exists, roughly how large it is, and when you saved changes. Use a strong password, since the encrypted file is public.

Does it cost anything?

No. Skylog is free and MIT-licensed, and GitHub Pages hosts your copy for free.

Can I use it without GitHub?

Yes. Download index.html and open it. It runs offline and keeps your log in that browser, without a password.

Can I take my data elsewhere?

Any time. The menu exports a myFlightradar24-compatible CSV or a complete JSON backup.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open your site in any mobile browser and unlock it with your password.
